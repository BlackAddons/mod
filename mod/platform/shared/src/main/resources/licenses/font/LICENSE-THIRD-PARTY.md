# Third-party fonts

The typefaces bundled in this jar and the terms they ship under. The fonts themselves are under
`assets/blackaddons/font/`; this notice sits outside `assets/` because Minecraft resource paths are
lowercase-only, and a licence filed as a resource is one the game logs an error over on every launch.
Same jar, which is what the OFL asks for.

`OFL.txt` beside this file is the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in full, as shipped with Courier Prime. The
same licence governs JetBrains Mono and Inter - the text is one and the same document, and what differs per font
is the copyright line, which is why both are stated below. The OFL asks that this text travel with the
fonts, which is why it is in the jar rather than only linked.

## GNU Unifont - `unifont.ttf`

Copyright Roman Czyborra, Paul Hardy and contributors (https://unifoundry.com/unifont/).
Licensed under the **GNU GPL version 2 or later, with the GNU Font Embedding Exception**.

That exception is the part that matters here: it is what allows the font to be embedded in a document
or application **without** that work becoming subject to the GPL. Do not drop it when copying this
notice around.

## Minecraft - `minecraft.ttf`

By Craftron Gaming, published on dafont as a free font - free to use and to redistribute, including
commercially. Checked by the maintainer.

---

**What the OFL asks of us, in short:** keep the copyright notice and the licence text with the font,
do not sell the font on its own, and do not release a modified version under the reserved font name.
Bundling it inside a mod that is itself distributed under other terms is expressly fine.
